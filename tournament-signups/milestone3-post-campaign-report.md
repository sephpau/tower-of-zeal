Across the three flagship tournaments (Amiko Legends, Beacon × Arbitrum, and Survival Competition), **402 total signups resolved to 290 unique wallets**, meaning a meaningful share of participants returned for more than one event.

| Participation | Unique Wallets | Share |

| Joined **all 3** tournaments | 28 | 10% |
| **Total unique** | **290** | 100% |

**Key retention takeaways:**
- **29% of participants returned** for a second or third tournament — evidence the campaign built a recurring player base, not just one-off sign-ups.
- **28 "core" wallets** (10%) participated in every flagship tournament, forming a committed on-chain community nucleus that persisted across all three months.
- Signup timing shows a consistent pattern each event: a large launch-day spike followed by a closing-day surge, indicating both strong top-of-funnel reach and effective deadline-driven re-engagement.
